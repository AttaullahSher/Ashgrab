    About Ashgrab

    What is this?

    Ashgrab is a free tool for saving videos. Paste a link from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X and many other apps — you'll see a little preview, then one tap downloads the video to your device. That's the whole thing.

    How it works

    When you paste a link, Ashgrab asks a free helper service to fetch the video and pass it to your device. If one helper is busy or down, Ashgrab quietly tries others — with different settings too — until one delivers. You don't have to do anything; it sorts itself out.

    Tip: on your phone, choose Add to Home Screen in your browser menu. Ashgrab then shows up in your Share menu, so you can share any video straight into it from the app you're watching in.

    Privacy

    No account, no sign-up, no tracking, no ads. Nothing you paste is saved anywhere — the "Recent" list lives only on your own device, and you can clear it anytime with one tap.

    Fair use

    Please only save videos you have the right to keep — your own posts, or content the creator is happy for you to download. Be kind to creators.

    For power users

    Busy hours can slow the free helpers down. Run your own helper and paste its address in settings — your downloads then always work, at full speed. The project is open source on GitHub.
